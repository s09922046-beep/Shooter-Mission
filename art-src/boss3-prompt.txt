【第 1 則:本體(附 boss3-body-template.png)】

Boss art for one fixed screen of a 2D side-scrolling pixel-art run-and-gun game. Use the attached guide image as an exact layout template. The result will be shrunk 3 times (to 512 x 341 pixels), so it must read clearly at that size.

Guide image legend:
- Magenta #FF00FF = empty. Keep every area you do not paint as flat solid magenta #FF00FF.
- Grey areas and the blue water band = drawn by the game. Keep them exactly as they are; draw nothing there.
- White dashed columns = climbable tentacles that the game draws later. Do not draw anything inside them.
- Orange box = THE BOSS BODY. Paint the creature inside this box only. It is cut off by the top edge of the image: most of its body is hidden above the screen, we only see its lower half.
- Red box = THE EYE. It must sit exactly inside the red box.

Creature: an original, deeply unsettling abyssal cephalopod horror that clings to the ceiling of an underground cavern. Its huge soft body is curled up tightly into itself, a heavy coiled mass of thick tentacles wrapped around each other in the center of the orange box, as if the whole creature is hunched and huddled. A few small round egg sacs are tucked between the coils (5 to 7 sacs, each one simple, translucent pale violet, with a faint dark dot inside).
Key identifying feature (the most important thing in the image): ONE single enormous eye in the middle of the body, set deep inside a horizontal fleshy eyelid slit, like the creature's whole body exists to hold this eye. The eye is wrong and disturbing:
- The eyeball is dull sickly off-white with a few thick dark veins.
- The iris is made of uneven concentric rings of golden yellow and dark amber.
- The pupil is a tall vertical slit, and from inside the pupil a bright yellow flame-like light pours outward, in 4 to 6 thick curling tongues that spill over the iris and onto the eyelids. Draw these flame tongues as big simple solid shapes, not soft glow.
- Yellow is used ONLY in the eye. Nothing else in the image is yellow, orange or red.
Colors of the body: dark violet flesh with deep plum shadows, the underside of the tentacles is pale pinkish violet with one row of round suckers. Wet, glossy look shown only by a few simple light highlight shapes. No green, no blue on the creature.
Do not draw any tentacle hanging below the orange box (the game draws the hanging tentacles).

Output rules:
- Same canvas size and aspect ratio as the guide (1536 x 1024), everything in exactly the same positions as the guide.
- Clean flat-color illustration with dark outlines of even thickness (about 4 pixels).
- 4 to 5 tones per material for large areas (light to dark steps). No gradients, no soft shading, no texture noise, no glow halo, no rim light, no particle effects.
- No shape or gap smaller than 12 pixels across. A few big readable shapes; no thin wisps, no fine wrinkles.
- The design must be original and must not resemble any existing game, film, anime, toy or brand character.

Background art for one fixed screen of a 2D side-scrolling pixel-art run-and-gun game: the boss arena of a giant tentacled eye creature. Use the attached guide image as an exact layout template. It will be shrunk 3 times (to 512 x 341 pixels), so it must read clearly at that size.

Scene: a deep underground cavern with a dark abyssal pool. Everything outside the boxes is painted (no magenta left except inside the yellow crack).
