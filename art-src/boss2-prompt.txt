【2 王 墮落女武神 希爾德:三則依序送,同一個對話】

【第 1 則:主設計圖】

Boss concept for a 2D side-scrolling pixel-art run-and-gun game. This image is a design reference that will later be redrawn as a small sprite about 40 pixels tall, so it must read clearly when shrunk 20 times.

Character: an original fallen valkyrie knight, a tall armored woman, full body, strict side view, FACING LEFT, standing in a ready stance.
Key identifying features (all three must be obvious in a pure black silhouette):
1. ONE large feathered wing on her back, swept up and back. The other wing is only a short broken stump.
2. A long straight two-handed longsword with a broad blade, almost as long as her body, held forward and low.
3. A closed helmet with a single swept-back metal fin on top. No wings on the helmet. No face visible.
Details:
- Helmet has one narrow horizontal visor slit that glows solid bright red. This slit is the ONLY red element in the whole image.
- Armor: tarnished, slightly oxidized dark silver plate (cool grey-silver, not gold, not black).
- Cloth: a grey-blue knee-length skirt or tabard under the armor, cut in 2 or 3 large simple panels.
- Wing feathers: grey-white, the wing tip charred black, drawn as 5 to 7 big separate feather shapes, not many small ones.
- Slender but sturdy proportions, about 6 heads tall. No visible hair, no cape, no prosthetic or mechanical arm.

Output rules:
- Canvas 1536 x 1024. The character (including wing and sword) fills about 80% of the canvas height, centered.
- Background is one flat solid magenta #FF00FF. No floor, no shadow, no text, no labels, no frame.
- Clean flat-color illustration with bold dark outlines of even thickness (about 8 pixels).
- 4 to 5 tones per material for large areas (light to dark steps). No gradients, no soft shading, no texture noise, no glow halo, no rim light, no particle effects.
- No shape or gap smaller than 40 pixels across. Prefer a few big readable shapes over many small details. No thin spikes, no fine engraving, no chains, no tattered cloth strips.
- The design must be original and must not resemble any existing game, film, anime, toy or brand character.

【第 2 則:姿勢表(第 1 張滿意後送出)】

Now draw exactly the same character, same design, same colors and same style, as a pose sheet: four full-body poses in one horizontal row, all FACING LEFT, all at the same scale, evenly spaced, on the same flat magenta #FF00FF background. No text, no labels, no motion lines, no effects.
1. Idle ready stance (same as before).
2. Wind-up: sword raised high over the shoulder, ready to swing down.
3. Lunge thrust: low forward dash, body leaning forward, sword pointed straight ahead horizontally at full reach.
4. Plunge: airborne, knees bent, sword held with both hands pointing straight down below her, about to strike the ground.
Keep the wing, broken stump and helmet fin visible in every pose. Each pose fits inside its own quarter of the canvas.

【第 3 則:剪影(第 1 張滿意後送出;可與第 2 則對調順序)】

Now output exactly the same design as the first image (the idle stance), same canvas and same position, as a pure black silhouette (#000000) on the flat magenta #FF00FF background. Only the visor slit stays red. No outlines, no interior details.
