【第 1 則:主設計圖】

Boss concept for a 2D side-scrolling pixel-art run-and-gun game. This image is a design reference that will later be redrawn as a small sprite about 46 pixels tall, so it must read clearly when shrunk 18 times.

Character: an original sinister lich sorcerer, floating in the air, full body, strict side view, FACING LEFT.
Key identifying features (all three must be obvious in a pure black silhouette):
1. A huge, wide, deep hood with a tall pointed peak that leans forward. Inside the hood is pure black emptiness; the face cannot be seen at all, only two tiny dim pale-green eye points.
2. A very broad, heavy robe that widens downward like a tall triangle and has no visible feet. The bottom of the robe breaks apart into 3 or 4 large, irregular, smoke-like blobs that float below it (big solid shapes, not thin wisps).
3. A faceted gem floating in front of the chest, not touching the robe, glowing solid bright red. This gem is the ONLY red element in the whole image.
Details:
- Robe: ink black with dark purple folds, drawn as a few large fold shapes.
- Hands: two long skeletal hands in bone ivory coming out of wide sleeves; the front hand is raised, fingers spread, casting a spell (no magic effect drawn).
- To the right of the lich, at about one quarter of its height, draw ONE separate small floating bone lantern: a cage made of a few thick bones with a small skull on top, a bright green soul flame inside. The green flame is drawn as one simple solid shape.
- No staff, no crown, no visible skull face, no jewelry other than the gem.

Output rules:
- Canvas 1536 x 1024. The lich fills about 80% of the canvas height, slightly left of center; the lantern is on the right side with clear empty space between them.
- Background is one flat solid magenta #FF00FF. No floor, no shadow, no text, no labels, no frame.
- Clean flat-color illustration with bold dark outlines of even thickness (about 8 pixels). Where the black robe touches the outline, use a dark purple outline so the shape stays readable.
- 4 to 5 tones per material for large areas (light to dark steps). No gradients, no soft shading, no texture noise, no glow halo, no rim light, no particle effects.
- No shape or gap smaller than 40 pixels across. Prefer a few big readable shapes over many small details. No thin spikes, no fine runes, no chains, no tattered cloth strips.
- The design must be original and must not resemble any existing game, film, anime, toy or brand character.

Now draw exactly the same lich, same design, same colors and same style, as a pose sheet: three full-body poses in one horizontal row, all FACING LEFT, all at the same scale, evenly spaced, on the same flat magenta #FF00FF background. Do not draw the lantern. No text, no labels, no effects.
