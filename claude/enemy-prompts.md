雜兵概念圖提示詞(11/2 定案,丙方案 5 系列)

用法:每個系列一則,整段程式碼框內文字貼給 ChatGPT(共同要求已併入每則),一次生成一張。生成後原圖交給 Claude,Claude 去背、縮小並改寫成像素矩陣(遊戲內精靈:地面兵 14×18、砲台與迫擊砲 16×18、飛行類 16×12)。 概念圖只當外形參考,配色會由 Claude 依各大關重新上色,所以不必追求最終顏色。若生成結果有文字、陰影底或漸層背景,請要求重生成。

共同要求(已併入下方每則,這裡只供參考)
Character concept sheet for a 2D side-scrolling run-and-gun game.
Background: one flat solid magenta color #FF00FF covering the whole image, no gradient, no floor, no shadows on the background, no text, no labels, no frames, no UI.
Every character is shown in strict side view facing RIGHT, full body, standing on an invisible common baseline, evenly spaced with clear magenta gaps between them so each can be cut out separately.
Style: clean flat-color illustration with bold dark outlines, 5 to 7 colors per character, no gradients, no soft shading, no rim lighting, no glow halos.
These designs will be shrunk to tiny pixel sprites (about 14 by 18 pixels), so every character needs a big readable silhouette, chunky limbs, an oversized head or single eye, and at most two or three small details. Avoid thin weapons, capes, fur or anything that disappears at small size.
Small glowing points (eyes, lenses, vents) are allowed but must be tiny; no red, orange or pink glow, those colors are reserved for enemy bullets.
All designs must be original and must not resemble any existing game, film, toy or brand character.
